EXP & Streak Guidelines
⚡ How EXP & Levels Work
Your financial journey is gamified to help you build consistent, healthy money habits. Every 50 XP advances you to the next Level (Novice → Builder → Tracker → Wealth Master).
🌟 How to Earn XP
+5 XP Log a Transaction: Every income, expense, or transfer logged.
+10 XP Zero-Expense Day: Automatically awarded for any past day with ₱0 expenses! You never need to click a manual button—the system detects it for you.
+10 XP Debt Payment: Making or recording a payment towards any debt.
+25 XP Settle a Debt: Completely clearing and settling an outstanding debt.
🔥 Daily Streak Rules
• A streak increments each day you stay mindful with your money.
• A day counts toward your streak if you either logged a transaction or had a disciplined zero-expense day.
• If you didn't spend anything yesterday, your streak is automatically protected and continued without penalty!
🛡️ Streak Freezes
Maintain a 7-day streak to earn a bonus Streak Freeze (holds up to 2). A freeze automatically protects your streak if a day is ever missed.
